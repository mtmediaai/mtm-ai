# Agentic Search Era

**Definition:** The current phase of search evolution in which AI systems move beyond returning links to synthesizing answers, making recommendations, and taking actions on behalf of users. Marked by Google I/O 2026 (May 19, 2026) as the inflection point.

## Context

The Agentic Search Era began when Google made AI Mode default for 1B+ users. Elizabeth Reid: "Google Search is AI Search through and through." Users no longer browse results — they delegate intent to agents that research, verify, and act. Visibility is no longer about ranking; it is about being the entity the agent trusts, cites, and recommends.

## Invisible Elite Example

A user asks: "Find me a commercial real estate attorney who handled $50M+ transactions in Texas." The agent doesn't return a list — it researches, filters by verified deal history, checks bar standing, and returns one recommendation with citations. Only businesses with structured deal evidence and verified authority survive this funnel.

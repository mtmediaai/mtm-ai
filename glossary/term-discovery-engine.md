# Discovery Engine

**Definition:** MTM's self-serve AI visibility audit and Solar Ascension conversion system. Transforms a cold prospect into a self-qualified buyer through a free AI Visibility Snapshot.

## Context

The Discovery Engine is MTM's front-door product. Prospects enter their business details and receive an instant AI Visibility Snapshot — a machine-generated assessment of how they appear (or don't) to AI systems. The output quantifies their AI Invisibility, AI Erasure risk, and AI Brand Ignorance score, then routes qualified prospects to the Diagnostic Chamber.

## Invisible Elite Example

A $4M/year wealth advisory firm enters the Discovery Engine. The Snapshot reveals: zero entity schema, no corroboration layer, 87% AI Invisibility score. The firm sees exactly why they're invisible. The engine then offers a 15-minute Diagnostic Chamber call to map the remediation path.

# Invisible Infrastructure Intelligence (I3)

**Definition:** The three-layer framework (Entity Clarity, Corroboration, Structured Discoverability) that translates a business's earned real-world reputation into the machine-readable infrastructure AI systems need to identify, verify, and recommend it.

## Context

I3 is the treatment for AI Brand Ignorance. It operates across three layers: **Entity Clarity** — AI systems can identify who you are, what you do, and whom you serve; **Corroboration** — third-party sources, structured data, and citations confirm your claims; **Structured Discoverability** — machine-readable infrastructure (schema, JSON-LD, entity graphs) makes you findable and recommendable. This is not SEO. It is the infrastructure that makes SEO work in the Agentic Search Era.

## Invisible Elite Example

A $6M/year M&A advisory firm with 50 closed deals and zero AI visibility. I3 builds: structured entity data defining their exact specialization, a corroboration layer of verified deal announcements and third-party citations, and schema infrastructure that makes every piece of their expertise machine-readable. When a founder asks "who handles $50M SaaS exits," the AI recommends them.

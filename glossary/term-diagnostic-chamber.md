# Diagnostic Chamber

**Definition:** The three-field intake form on the Palace where qualified prospects submit for a 15-minute diagnostic call. Creates HubSpot contact, logs to Supabase, checks territory availability.

## Context

The Diagnostic Chamber is the first human touchpoint after the Discovery Engine. Qualified prospects (those with sufficient revenue, established reputation, and clear AI visibility gaps) submit a brief intake. The system creates a HubSpot contact, logs the request to Supabase for the Golden Legion, and checks territory availability before confirming the 15-minute diagnostic call.

## Invisible Elite Example

A $5M/year M&A advisory firm receives their AI Visibility Snapshot showing 92% invisibility. They click "Book Diagnostic Chamber," fill three fields (name, email, revenue range), and the system confirms territory availability and schedules a 15-minute call with a MTM strategist.

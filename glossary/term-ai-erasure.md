# AI Erasure

**Definition:** The practical displacement of a business from AI-mediated answers or recommendations when stronger, clearer, more widely corroborated, or more accessible information about alternatives is surfaced instead. The business is not ranked poorly — it is replaced in the answer entirely.

## Context

AI Erasure is the second half of the Invisible Elite problem. Even if a business has some digital presence, AI systems synthesize answers from the *strongest* available signals. A competitor with better-structured data, more citations, and clearer entity definitions will be surfaced instead — not because they're better, but because they're more legible to the machine. The Invisible Elite firm is erased from the answer.

## Invisible Elite Example

A $5M/year structural engineering firm with 30 years of bridge and infrastructure projects. A younger competitor with an aggressive content strategy and structured project schema appears in 90% of AI answers for "structural engineer for infrastructure projects." The established firm isn't ranked lower — it's not in the answer at all. Erased.

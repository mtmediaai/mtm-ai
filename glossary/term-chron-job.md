# CHRON JOB

**Definition:** MTM's market intelligence and SEO 2.0 command division. Runs weekly surveillance scans, monthly synthesis, and maintains the freshness loop for all canonical knowledge assets.

## Context

CHRON JOB ensures MTM's canonical knowledge base never drifts. Weekly: algorithm change detection, new discovery engine features, competitive shifts. Monthly: glossary clarity review, timeline accuracy, validation page sync, Palace content freshness. Every change follows the promotion gate: internal draft → Nina Filter (boundary) → Fetti (claims) → Architect approval → manual commit to public repo.

CHRON JOB detects the shift within 48 hours, updates the "citation-worthy evidence" guidance in the glossary, triggers Aurelius to prioritize video atomization for active clients, and logs the protocol adjustment in the internal vault.

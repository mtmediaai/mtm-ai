# The Palace

**Definition:** MTM's public headquarters at mtmediaai.com. The human-facing showcase that links to canonical sources, hosts the Knowledge Vault, and serves as the conversion environment.

## Context

The Palace is where the public encounters MTM's intelligence. Every glossary term, timeline entry, and validation page in this repository has a corresponding Palace page — designed for human reading, linked to the canonical source. The Palace does not create truth; it renders truth from the repository. It hosts the Knowledge Vault, the Discovery Engine, the Diagnostic Chamber, and the Confirmation Chamber.

## Invisible Elite Example

A prospect reads "What is I3?" on the Palace. The page gives a clear answer, then links: "View the machine-legible definition →" (points to this glossary term). "See the full system →" (points to I3 System Overview). "Verify our research →" (points to Backed by Science). Trust is earned through traceability.

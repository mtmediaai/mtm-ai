# Aurelius

**Definition:** MTM's content distribution and atomization system. Takes one canonical asset and produces platform-specific outputs across LinkedIn, Medium, Spark Pages, social, and the Chronicle.

## Context

Aurelius solves the distribution-fragmentation problem. A single pillar asset (e.g., an I3 Diagnostic Report) becomes: a LinkedIn article with schema, a Medium post with canonical links, a Spark Page for visual storytelling, social threads with entity tags, and a Chronicle entry for the Knowledge Vault. Every fragment carries structural signal back to the canonical source.

## Invisible Elite Example

An M&A advisory firm's "Cross-Border Deal Structure" pillar asset enters Aurelius. Output: 12 platform-native pieces. LinkedIn drives referral partners. Medium captures search intent. Spark Pages convert visual learners. Social threads reinforce entity associations. The Chronicle archives the canonical version. Each piece cites the source.

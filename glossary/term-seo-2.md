# SEO 2.0

**Definition:** The evolution of search optimization from keyword-ranking to entity clarity, structured data, and AI-citation readiness. Adopted and operationalized by MTM starting 2022; not coined by MTM.

## Context

Traditional SEO optimized for link-based ranking. SEO 2.0 optimizes for **entity clarity** — making a business unmistakably identifiable to AI systems — **structured data** that machines can parse, and **citation readiness** so discovery engines can reference the business with confidence. MTM adopted this framing in 2022 when Perplexity signaled the shift; we did not coin the term, but we operationalized it for the Invisible Elite.

## Invisible Elite Example

A $4M/year tax advisory firm specializing in cross-border M&A. Their old SEO targeted "tax services [city]." SEO 2.0 restructures their digital presence around the entity "Cross-Border M&A Tax Advisory," with structured schema, verified third-party citations, and content that answers the specific questions AI systems get asked — so when a founder asks Perplexity "who handles cross-border M&A tax for SaaS exits," this firm is the cited answer.

# AI Invisibility

**Definition:** The condition in which a business has insufficient, fragmented, inaccessible, or weakly contextualized information for AI-assisted discovery systems to reliably identify and understand it. The business exists — the machine cannot see it clearly enough to recommend it.

## Context

AI Invisibility is the first half of the Invisible Elite problem. The business has real-world credibility, but its digital footprint is too sparse, too unstructured, or too human-centric for AI systems to parse. No entity record. No structured expertise signals. No machine-readable proof. The machine literally cannot see the business clearly enough to include it in an answer.

## Invisible Elite Example

A 15-year commercial litigation firm with 200+ verdicts and settlements. Their website has attorney bios and practice area pages — but no structured data, no case schema, no verified third-party citations. When a GC asks Perplexity "best commercial litigation firm for breach of contract in [state]," the firm does not appear. Not because they're not qualified — because they're invisible to the machine.

# The Forge

**Definition:** MTM's four-agent AI council: Goldie (Spark/ADOPT), Roman (Spine/ENHANCE), Nina (Shield/DEPLOY), Echo (Shadow/PULSE BACK). The public-facing intelligence layer of MTM's content and client experience.

## Context

The Forge is the intelligence layer behind MTM's public output. Goldie sparks new ideas and adopts external signals (ADOPT). Roman builds the structural spine and enhances them (ENHANCE). Nina shields against error and deploys with rigor (DEPLOY). Echo shadows the work and pulses back to ensure the human experience survives (PULSE BACK). Their deliberations produce the content and protocols that define MTM's public voice. The internal architecture remains private.

## Invisible Elite Example

A prospect asks: "Why does Perplexity cite my competitor?" The Forge activates: Goldie maps the future where the prospect is cited (ADOPT). Roman designs the entity-structure path (ENHANCE). Nina identifies the evidence gaps the prospect will resist filling (DEPLOY). Echo designs the client journey so the work feels like partnership, not homework (PULSE BACK).

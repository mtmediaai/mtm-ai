# Confirmation Chamber

**Definition:** The final shared checkpoint before paid engagement. Reviews diagnostic findings, confirms fit, and routes the client to the appropriate Solar Ascension tier.

## Context

The Confirmation Chamber is the last step before a client enters the Solar Ascension program. After the 15-minute diagnostic call, the strategist presents findings, confirms mutual fit, and routes the client to the appropriate tier based on their revenue, visibility gap, and growth objectives. No client enters without passing through the Confirmation Chamber.

## Invisible Elite Example

The M&A advisory firm completes their diagnostic call. The strategist confirms: $5M revenue, 92% AI Invisibility, clear entity clarity gap. Fit confirmed. The firm is routed to Solar Ascension Tier 2 (Entity Clarity + Corroboration). Engagement begins.
